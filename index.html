Poetry

Words

My Tambre Vessel

My tambre vessel stands against the rhythm of the world Crash against the beat of the tide Ride those arid waves of feckless harmonies And find comfort in the dissonance

Vine Grip

Upon shimmering reflection i refer to the light of figments reaching out to touch the moon on a cold stillwind day leaves befell the underbrush like green and brown carpets bristles in the gust and tickles my lust here we lay our memories of vine grip pinpricks I say unto my ancient bring me back to reality

Numbers

I am silence in the mirror one crutch for my fractured mind bedbugs keeping me up at night fight so fractally real shouting voices without tongues but with artillery fire and glass canon guns that shatter words into blank pages where they remain mute forever dead songs for headstone hillsides where rain dare not fall

Landfill

For the first time trying on open eyes costing nothing but alien teardrops i hope you found the piece you were looking for. There was hope in the landfill once drunken faith, defiled for your new suit conclusion. Was there never more thread on the endless spool of lies you were fed as a child. No medicine to lead the way like soldiers on the line deep into the fray, i wonder. Can you see me clearly now Standing tall, yet crooked Finger pointing upwards Brown tipped like rolling swine I beg you to smell the poison What poison? this is wine!

Biography

About

I am a musician, poet and artist from the southwest of Norway. I study Digital Culture at the University of Bergen.

Former Bassist in MovePrimer and OCM. Current demiurge behind Color for a Blind Man, Cull the Living Flower, The Wayfarer Project and one half of Rathaus.

As i am on my last year on the masters program, I spend most of my time writing, or thats at least what i tell my supervisor. Don't tell her please
